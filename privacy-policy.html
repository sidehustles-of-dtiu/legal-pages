Privacy policy

Draft, written 2026-10-16. Not legal advice. Have it reviewed before you submit it to a platform.

Who this covers

Studio is a private tool that one person runs on their own computer, for their own channels. It is not offered to other users, and it has no public website or public sign up. "We" below means the owner of the tool.

What is stored, and where

  • Source videos, clips, transcripts, captions and the owner's edits and notes are stored on the owner's PC, in the library folder. They are not uploaded anywhere except to a platform the owner chooses to post to.
  • Results from each platform (views, watch time, followers, revenue) are stored in the same database, so the tool can show them and learn from them.
  • Platform sign-in tokens are stored in the database on the PC. They are never part of the code, a log or an export.

What is shared

  • A clip is sent to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram only after the owner approves it and presses a button, or at a time the owner scheduled.
  • Transcripts and ranking run on the PC. No audio, video or transcript is sent to a cloud service.
  • By default the language model runs on the PC. If the owner turns on the ChatGPT option in Settings, the prompts sent to it, which can include clip titles, results and ideas, and the questions typed into the chat, are sent to OpenAI through the owner's own ChatGPT account. The tool says so next to the setting.

Data from platforms

When the owner connects a platform, the tool asks only for the permissions it needs to post and to read the results of the owner's own posts. It does not read other people's accounts, comments or private messages.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can export everything the tool stores, and can delete clips, transcripts, analytics and platform tokens at any time from Settings. Deleting is immediate. Source video files in the sources folder are the owner's own files and are never touched by the tool.
