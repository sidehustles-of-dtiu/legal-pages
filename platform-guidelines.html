Platform guidelines

The rules this tool is built to follow, in plain words. Draft, 2026-10-16. Check each platform's current rules before relying on this.

Every platform

  • Use only videos you have permission to use, and keep the record.
  • Add your own voice or commentary to reused clips. Slideshow-only and mass-produced videos may not earn money and can lose reach.
  • Do not pose as an expert on health, law, finance or politics.
  • Do not post anything until you have approved it.

YouTube

  • Shorts can be up to three minutes. The tool caps clips at 180 seconds.
  • A new API project may be limited to private uploads until it passes Google's audit.
  • From 1 February 2027 the Partner Program bar for new applicants is higher. Accept the updated terms by 31 January 2027.

TikTok

  • Posts from an app TikTok has not audited are private only.
  • Short clips may not count toward creator rewards, which look at videos over one minute. Check eligibility in TikTok Studio.

Instagram

  • Posting through the API needs a professional account and Meta's app review.
